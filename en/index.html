FAQ

Frequently asked
questions

What is ROCky?

ROCky (Roblox Online Checker) is a Telegram bot for monitoring Roblox player statuses and gaming activity. It sends status-change notifications, collects session history and shows activity statistics.

Does it need my Roblox password?

No. Start with a username, numeric player ID or profile link. Do not share a password, login code or .ROBLOSECURITY cookie with the bot.

How much does it cost?

You can start monitoring for free. Slots and available features depend on your user level. Use /profile and /level inside the bot to see current terms.

Can I follow several players?

Yes, if your user level includes multiple monitoring slots. Use /profile to see your limit and /level to view the features available at your level.

Can I see activity from before monitoring began?

ROCky saves observations from the moment monitoring starts. It cannot reconstruct a complete history for periods when the bot was not following the player.

Why is a player offline or their game unknown?

Information depends on Roblox privacy settings and available data. Hidden statuses, game names and servers cannot always be identified. Updates and notifications may be delayed.

Is ROCky affiliated with Roblox?

ROCky is an independent project. It is not an official service of Roblox Corporation or Telegram.
